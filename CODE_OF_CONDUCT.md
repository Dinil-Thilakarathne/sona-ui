# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Sona UI community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Give and receive feedback with empathy.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct
that is not professional or welcoming are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code,
issues, and other community activity that does not align with this Code of
Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent
ban from the project spaces.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately through GitHub's private reporting channel for this
repository. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.
